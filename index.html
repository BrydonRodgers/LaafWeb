Common questions

Short, straight answers.

How long does it take?

Usually a few days after we have your details and photos. We show you a preview first.

Is there a contract?

No. The monthly fee can be cancelled at any time. See our terms for the detail.

Why R79 a month?

It covers hosting, the secure connection and one small change a month (like a new photo or phone number).

Do I get my own domain name?

Your site gets a free address. Your own .co.za domain can be added at cost (from about R150 a year).

How do I pay?

By EFT or card. For the monthly fee we send a secure payment link, or give you our bank details.

Who is LaafWeb?

A local service of BROD Traders (Pty) Ltd in Langebaan. You talk directly to the people who build your website.
